"Here's how the AI assistant works. A visitor asks a question — let's say, 'What did Ambedkar say about caste?'

Step 1: We detect the language and extract key entities. Step 2: We search both semantically and by keyword across our vector database and full-text index. Step 3: A reranker picks the top 5 most relevant passages from Ambedkar's verified writings. Step 4: Llama 3 generates a factual answer with citations. Step 5: We display the answer with clickable source references. Step 6: Text-to-speech narrates it in the visitor's language.

The entire pipeline — from question to spoken answer — takes under 3 seconds. And every answer is sourced. No hallucinations."

About This Archive

Samdarshi is an AI-powered digital heritage archive dedicated to preserving, digitizing, and making accessible the legacy of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar — the architect of the Indian Constitution, champion of social justice, and one of India's greatest thinkers.

Key Features

🤖AI Research Assistant with verified citations
🌐Multilingual: English, Hindi, Marathi
📅Interactive Timeline: 1891–1956
📜Manuscript viewer with OCR overlay
🔍OCR Digitization Pipeline
🔊Text-to-Speech in all languages

Technology

Built with open-source technologies: FastAPI, Llama 3, pgvector, Tesseract OCR, EasyOCR, Electron. All components are MIT-licensed.

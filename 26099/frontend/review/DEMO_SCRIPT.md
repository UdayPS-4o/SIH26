## Presenter Notes

- **Speed: fast.** Each page gets 15–30 seconds max. Cut before the audience gets bored.
- **Voiceover throughout.** Presenter does not need to narrate every click — let the screen speak.
- **Show, don't tell.** The scoring arithmetic, the four-names panel, and the live import are the moments that sell. Let them breathe for 5 extra seconds each.
- **The Overview panel 02 is the hero shot.** It is the one thing judges should remember. Give it the most screen time.
- **End on the Dashboard.** Full numbers, full charts. Leaves the impression of a finished product, not a prototype in progress.

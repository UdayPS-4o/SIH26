## TIPS FOR RECORDING

1. **Use a screen recording tool** like OBS Studio, Loom, or the built-in Windows Game Bar (Win + G).
2. **Record the mouse cursor** -- make sure it is visible and moves smoothly. Do not rush clicks.
3. **Pause 1-2 seconds** between scenes to let animations (shimmer, counter, chart renders) play out.
4. **Narration**: Record voiceover separately if possible, then sync in video editor. This gives cleaner audio.
5. **If recording in one take**: Speak slightly slower than normal conversation. Leave micro-pauses at scene transitions.
6. **The app's loading shimmer** (1.5 seconds) will play on first navigation -- factor this into timing or reload the page before recording to ensure it has cached.
7. **For the Simulator slider drag**: Move it slowly so the risk gauge animation is clearly visible on screen.
8. **End frame**: Hold on the Dashboard hero for a full 3-4 seconds after finishing narration so it does not feel abrupt.

## Presenter Tips

1. **Maximise the browser window** before recording. The sidebar and cards are designed for full-screen viewing — a smaller window will cut off content.
2. **Use the sidebar for all navigation.** Every page in the flow is reachable from the left nav. Click smoothly; do not refresh the page between scenes.
3. **Mention these are simulated/demo values.** All animal data, sensor readings, and risk scores in this prototype are synthetic. The production system will connect to real IoT sensors and farm management APIs.
4. **The Simulator is the "wow" moment — spend extra time there.** Judges want to see interactivity. Drag each slider slowly, one at a time, so the audience can see the gauge respond. Pause on the Vet Escalation card for at least two seconds.
5. **Click through smoothly, no hesitation.** The flow is Dashboard → Animals → Animal Detail → Simulator → Herd Intelligence → Detection → Dashboard. Practice the clicks once or twice before recording so transitions feel natural.
6. **Pace:** aim for 160–170 words per minute. If you rush, you will hit the 90-second mark before finishing Scene 5. If you go slower, trim Scene 4 slightly.
